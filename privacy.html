Privacy policy

Simba Home Assistant · Last updated 8 October 2026

What this app is

A private Home Assistant installation used by one household. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it uses

With your permission, the app uses the Google Sheets and Google Drive APIs only to create one spreadsheet in your Google Drive and add rows of household expense information to it (date, description, amount, who paid and running balances). It does not read, change or delete any other files in your Google Drive.

Where data is stored

Expense information is stored on the household's own Home Assistant computer and in the spreadsheet in your own Google Drive. It is not sold, shared with, or sent to anyone else.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the spreadsheet from Google Drive.
